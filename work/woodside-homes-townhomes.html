Woodside Townhomes

The Day Ranch Community in Bluffdale, Utah, developed by Woodside Homes, showcases townhouse prototypes crafted by Knighton Architecture through collaborative design charrettes with the Woodside Homes team. These thoughtfully designed units have proven highly popular, achieving strong sales and serving as a model for new developments in communities across Utah.
