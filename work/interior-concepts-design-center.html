Interior Concepts Design House

Completed in 2019, the Interior Concepts Design House serves as the headquarters for Interior Concepts, a leading local interior design firm. The facility also offers meeting spaces and material sample resources for other local designers, fostering collaboration and a community-centered approach to interior design.

Behind the main offices, a warehouse supports the firm's services by providing storage for furnishings and materials. The design goal was to create a space that feels like home, reflects the creativity and professionalism of the Interior Concepts team, and inspires innovation and collaboration.
