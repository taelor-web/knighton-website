Fifth Ward Chapel | SLC UT

The Fifth Ward Chapel in Salt Lake City is a historic architectural project focused on the reconstruction of a cherished community landmark which was originally built in 1910. The current project aims to restore the chapel to its 1910 state, adhering closely to the original design and architectural details that characterized the early 20th-century structure. This reconstruction effort not only seeks to preserve the historical integrity of the building but also to bring new life into its abandoned state, ensuring it is a vibrant part of the community's landscape.
