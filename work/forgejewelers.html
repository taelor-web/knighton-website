Forge Jewelry Works

Breathing new life into a historic building with strong architectural character, the Forge Jewelry Works headquarters underwent a full interior renovation and exterior transformation. The result is a vibrant home for a business that celebrates craftsmanship—where skilled jewelsmiths create custom pieces in full view of their clients.

Founded by a local Utah Valley family, Forge Jewelry Works has grown into a thriving creative venture. Their investment in redeveloping this property not only supports their expanding business but also adds renewed energy to the heart of downtown Provo.

Construction was completed in Spring 2025, and the space now stands as a testament to both the artistry of its occupants and the lasting value of revitalizing historic structures.
