Beaver, UT

The Creamery

Each of our projects at Knighton Architecture has a story that matters to the business owner, so it matters to us. A few years ago, the iconic cheese plant in Beaver was in need of a refresh. The local managers had a vision of expanding the small cheese store attached to the plant, and the Dairy Farmers of America (DFA) leadership saw the potential to create a unique experience that brings the farm directly to the table.

What was once a small building to the side of a dairy processing plant, became a beautiful new building with more customers than anyone could have imagined. The new store features expanded retail space, a cafe, and an interactive, educational dairy experience space. It’s had a massive positive impact on the local community as well as the family farmers represented by Dairy Farmers of America, telling their story of bringing fresh dairy products from farm to table. This is only one illustration of how our process works – which begins with an idea and ends with positive impacts on communities.
