The Valley View Residence | Provo UT

Perched on one of the highest lots in the foothills of Provo is a Modern Southern California inspired home, with breathtaking views of the Utah Valley. Starting up top with a spacious rooftop deck. The primary suite offers 2 full window walls. With indoor outdoor living on the main and lower level you can watch TV, enjoy company, and it opens up to the expansive outdoor space, where additional entertaining can take place. Massive theater room, bunk room, and gym located in the basement. The backyard offers a place to relax with a fire pit, BBQ, hot tub and after you enjoy those amenities you can recover with access to a-cold plunge and sauna that is tucked away off the back yard.
