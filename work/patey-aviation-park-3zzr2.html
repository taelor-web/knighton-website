Payson Passive House

Introducing our first custom home in Payson, Utah, designed to achieve Passive House certification. This modern two-story residence exemplifies sustainable living through innovative passive heating and cooling strategies, significantly reducing energy consumption while ensuring year-round comfort.

The home features a hyper-insulated building envelope, maintaining optimal indoor temperatures and providing exceptional air quality. Its efficient layout maximizes space and functionality, highlighted by a unique cantilever design that doubles as a carport, optimizing the use of the site.

Built to last, this home not only prioritizes energy efficiency but also offers a comfortable and healthy living environment, setting a new standard for sustainable residential design.
