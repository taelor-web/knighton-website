Who we are

Meaningful design driven by real relationships

Knighton Architecture is a team of designers and creators dedicated to shaping a better built environment. We focus on meaningful, lasting relationships with our clients, so we can collaborate well and bring great places to life.

Too often, the design process feels confusing, stressful and expensive. We believe it should be the opposite: rewarding, inspiring and enjoyable, at every step.

Our story

A practice built on relationships

Jeff Knighton spent his early career studying and working in the Pacific Northwest. He came home to Utah to be closer to family and to build a different kind of firm: one that takes on creative, meaningful work, treats every client as a long-term relationship and feels like a family to the people inside it.

Rather than specialize in one building type, we chose to specialize in trust. Clients kept coming back, and the work grew with them, from restaurants and retail to housing, aviation, custom homes and historic buildings.

  1. 2015Rodizio Grill brings us on for its new restaurants, a partnership that now spans Orlando to San Diego.
  2. 2016We redesign the historic Madison Building on Center Street in downtown Provo.
  3. 2022The team grows, adding the project leads who run much of our work today.
  4. TodayEight people and a headquarters in Provo.
