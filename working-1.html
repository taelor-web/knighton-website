STEP 1 | Project Discovery

Before we start designing, we gather the information needed to begin the project. We meet with you to understand your needs, expectations, end goals, budgets, and any constraints you might have. We also study local zoning codes and regulations. If circumstances permit, we’d love to visit the site to help us understand the opportunities and challenges of your unique location.

STEP 2 | Concept Design

Once we understand the ideas and needs behind the project, we start with early conceptual design. We share ideas, explore possibilities, and give you a preview of what the project could ultimately look like. If the project is feasible and meets your needs, we will move to the next phase. Occasionally, it becomes clear that a project does not fit the site, or vice versa. In those instances, we will collaboratively tweak the design until you feel ready to proceed to the next phase.

STEP 3 | Construction Documents

This is where the real fun (and work) starts. We delve into the details of the design and carefully address all the complex elements that come together to bring the project to life. We offer digital 3D renderings and virtual walkthroughs to help you visualize and imagine what the project will look like when it's completed. Our team collaborates with various professional engineers who provide the technical specifications necessary to ensure the project is buildable. Additionally, we prepare the drawings and documents for city approval and guide you through that process.

STEP 4 | Construction Administration

At this point, your project truly comes to life. We provide support throughout construction to advocate for the owner, working closely with the contractor to coordinate and resolve any issues that arise during the building process. Our goal is to ensure that the project unfolds according to the plans. We remain engaged and by your side until the project is ready for you to move in.

HOW WE APPROACH PRICING/FEES

Pricing for professional design services can be difficult to predict due to the numerous variables inherent to each project. The descriptions below provide a framework for how we typically approach our fee structures. However, we recommend reaching out and scheduling an initial design consultation so we can review the details together, allowing us to provide a detailed fee proposal for your unique project.

Commercial | Multi-Family | Institutional

Typically 3%–7% of Expected Construction Costs

Cost Contributing Factors:

  • Additional engineering services. Civil, Structural, MEP Engineering, Landscape and/or Interior Design.
  • Project size, complexity and timeframe

*Proposals are prepared individually per project

Custom Residential

Typically $3–$7* per sq ft of Livable Space

Cost Contributing Factors:

  • Additional engineering services. Civil, Structural, MEP Engineering, Landscape and/or Interior Design.
  • Required additional approvals such as HOA Design Review, etc.

*Proposals are prepared individually per project

Master-planning & Site Planning

Typically $1K–$2K per acre of Developable Land

Cost Contributing Factors:

  • Additional engineering services. Civil, Structural, MEP Engineering, Landscape and/or Interior Design.
  • Required additional approvals

*Proposals are prepared individually per project
